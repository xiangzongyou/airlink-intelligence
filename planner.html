Flights are the nonstop routes of June 2014 (OpenFlights), with seats from BTS T-100. Times are estimates, not schedules: flying at about 460 mph plus 35 minutes per flight, 1 h 15 per connection, 2 hours for check-in and leaving the airport, and driving at about 52 mph over 1.2 × the straight-line distance (Lower 48 only). Trains are Amtrak's published schedule (GTFS, November 2026) and buses are the UIUC Intercity Bus GTFS (2025, about 70 operators), so ground options mix today's trains and buses with 2014 flights. Ground times are the scheduled ride times plus 20 minutes at the first station and 45 minutes per change, and assume you catch a departure: check the timetable, as some routes run once a day.
